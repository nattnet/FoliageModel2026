#3 — what each predictor measures
R red‑potential: capacity to manufacture crimson — cool above‑freezing nights (≈0–7 °C), bright days, trapped sugars, moisture, red‑capable species. Warm nights cap it (the El Niño effect).
Y yellow‑potential: carotenoid unmasking via a gradual cool‑down + gold species; hard freeze → brown.
C conditions: favourable and safe weather — low rain/wind leaf‑loss, low cloud, low abrupt frost = the booking‑risk axis.

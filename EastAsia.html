Section 1 · Method

How the model works — read this first

Everything below is driven by these definitions. The symbols R Y C H S are the only abbreviations used anywhere; everything else is spelled out.

The three lenses

  • Timing (Section 4) — does your window catch the peak, or land on green / bare?
  • Beauty (Section 3) — the realised look on the hill, scored 0–10 as a coloured dot.
  • Why (Section 3) — the forward‑looking predictors, and the Diagnosis pill that names any gap.

The beauty score & its bands

beauty score = ROUND( (w_H·Hue + w_S·Saturation + w_C·Cleanliness) / 10 , 1 )

The weights (w_H, w_S, w_C) are your dial in Section 2. The score maps to a colour:

≥ 9 — red — exceptional, peak, optimal
≥ 7 — orange — good, solid colour
≥ 5 — yellow — fair, muted or timing mismatch
≥ 3 — grey — poor, post‑peak or declining
< 3 — black — bare / snow / winter

The dial weights (Hue / Saturation / Cleanliness)

  • Hue — more = reward reds & a varied mix; less = a single colour scores like a varied one.
  • Saturation — more = reward pure, glowing colour; less = pale / hazy colour isn't penalised.
  • Cleanliness — more = penalise dull / brown / cloud harder; less = dullness matters less.

Default 45 / 45 / 10: hue and glow share the pie equally (I won't guess your taste); cleanliness is a small penalty / tiebreaker.

The predictors (R / Y / C), 0–10

  • R red‑potential — capacity to make crimson: cool above‑freezing nights (≈0–7 °C), bright days, trapped sugars, moisture, red species. Warm nights cap it.
  • Y yellow‑potential — carotenoid gold via a gradual cool‑down + gold species; a hard freeze turns it brown.
  • C conditions — favourable and safe weather: low rain/wind leaf‑loss, low cloud, low abrupt frost = the booking‑risk axis.

Reading the Diagnosis pill

The pill names the gap between potential and what's actually there:

aligned ✔ window sits on the peak.

rising · pre‑peak building, not yet there.

timing miss · still green leaf hasn't turned — weather isn't the problem.

past peak · still vivid / fair on the way down but colour remains (a high dot here is correct).

on peak · weather risk great now, one front could strip it.

off‑window · bare nothing left to see.

Baselines & ENSO (El Niño–Southern Oscillation) 2026

30‑year normal 1991–2020 (WMO — World Meteorological Organization); alternative tourism median 1981–2010. Drift studies: temperate China 1982–2011 (+0.12 days/year later end‑of‑season); subtropical China 2001–2018 (+0.41 days/year later onset); Japanese alps 2011–2020 (declining brightness).

2026 ENSO: IRI (International Research Institute for Climate and Society) ≈100% El Niño from the Jul–Aug–Sep trimester; NOAA/CPC (Climate Prediction Center) ≈97% persistence to spring 2027; consensus peak of the Niño‑3.4 index (the standard El Niño sea‑temperature region) ≈3.6 °C; ≈81% chance of very‑strong in Oct–Dec. Direction robust; magnitude re‑check 6–8 weeks out.
